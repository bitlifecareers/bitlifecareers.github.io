Some BitLife careers are available straight after secondary school, while others require university, Graduate School, Medical School, Dental School, Nursing School, or previous work experience.

This database gives you a quick reference for popular jobs and helps you plan your education before spending years on the wrong path.

The basics

How Careers Work in BitLife

Most standard jobs appear under the Occupation section once your character is old enough to work.

Getting hired does not depend on education alone. Employers can consider your character’s education, experience, stats, work performance, and decisions when evaluating job applications and promotions.

Some careers begin with an entry-level position. You may first need to apply for a junior, trainee, associate, or apprentice role and work your way up.

Community references also track Career Collections. A career is generally counted after your character remains in that profession for 20 consecutive years.

Start working sooner

Careers That Need No University Degree

You do not need university for every career. Several jobs can be started without a degree:

  • Baker
  • Bartender
  • Auto Mechanic
  • Baggage Handler
  • Beekeeper
  • Retail Salesperson
  • Cashier
  • Fisherman
  • Security Guard
  • Construction Worker

These jobs are useful when you want your character to start earning money early. They can also help in challenges where you need employment rather than a specialised profession.

Build the right foundation

Careers That Need University

University opens the door to many specialised careers. The important part is choosing the right major, not simply earning any degree.

Accounting can lead toward Accountant and Banker. Architecture is the route for Architect. Biology opens science-related paths. Computer Science can lead toward Animator and technology professions. Graphic Design can help with Fashion Designer.

If you know which career you want, choose your university subject around that goal. A degree does not automatically qualify your character for every professional job.

Advanced education

Medical Careers

Medical careers usually require more preparation. Brain Surgeon and Family Physician require Medical School, while nursing follows its own Nursing School route. Dentist requires Dental School.

Because these paths can take many in-game years, decide on them early.

Enlist or become an officer

Climb the ladder

Careers With Promotion Paths

Many professions work like career ladders. This can happen in Animator, Architect, Fashion Designer, Marine Biologist, Firefighter, Nursing, corporate careers, and mortuary careers.

The job list may first show a junior or entry-level version. Take the lower position, maintain strong performance, and work toward promotions. Changing professions too quickly can interrupt Career Collection progress.

Troubleshooting

What If the Job Does Not Appear?

Available job listings can vary, so meeting the education requirement does not always mean the exact position appears immediately.

  1. Confirm the correct degree or professional school.
  2. Look for a junior, trainee, associate, or apprentice version.
  3. Age up and check available jobs again.
  4. Recheck the Occupation section after the list changes.
  5. Check for another requirement such as experience or a licence.
  6. Check whether your character’s stats suit the career.
  7. Consider whether previous decisions or a criminal record affect the application.

Work backward

Plan Your Education Before Choosing a Career

First decide: Which career do I want? Then check: What education does that career require?

Choose the correct major, Graduate School, Medical School, or other professional education. This prevents years spent studying something that does not help with your target profession.

If your goal is Brain Surgeon, prepare for the medical route early instead of trying to change direction much later.

A route in practice

Career Planning Example

How to Become an Animator

  1. Finish secondary school.
  2. Apply to university.
  3. Choose Computer Science.
  4. Graduate.
  5. Check full-time jobs.
  6. Look for Animator or an entry-level version.
  7. Apply and pass the interview.
  8. Keep performance high.
  9. Stay in the profession and work toward promotions.

Community career references list Computer Science as the university requirement for Animator. The same planning method works for many professional careers.

Know the difference

Regular Careers vs Special Careers

Normal occupations use education, applications, interviews, work performance, and promotions. Special Careers or Job Packs can have their own mechanics, with examples including Actor, Mafia, Pro Athlete, Politician, and Musician.

Business also has its own system where characters can launch or acquire companies. These paths are better covered in dedicated guides.

Play smarter

Useful Career Tips

  • Choose your career before selecting a university major.
  • Keep Smarts high for education-heavy professions.
  • Do not assume every career requires university.
  • Search for a junior, trainee, associate, or apprentice version.
  • Stay in one profession for promotions or a Career Collection.
  • A correct degree makes you eligible but does not guarantee an immediate listing.
